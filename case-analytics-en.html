Analytics: from Power BI to dashboards built by each user

How we tackled a serious adoption problem in a tool that is essential for partners, replacing rigid reports with a customizable experience built without code.

RoleSenior Product Design Manager
CompanyBEES
Period2026
ScopeDashboards, KPIs and datasets for partners

Context

Analytics is where partners see the results of their actions with BEES as an e-commerce channel. It is an essential tool for them.

About 90% of it was built in Power BI, with visual and experience limitations. The result was a serious adoption problem.

First response

In the short term, we created a design guideline adapted to Power BI, so reports would have the look and feel of the company's design system.

It helped with visual consistency, but the experience was still frustrating. The limit was in the tool, not just in how it looked.

My role

I defined the strategy together with the group product manager (GPM) and led the designer who worked end to end on defining the project.

Discovery and product vision

Together with the product team, we ran discovery and product vision work to understand where Analytics could bring real value.

The main finding: Analytics has many different users. Each one's purpose, and the actions they take, vary with their profile and the data they can access.

What changes from one user to another
  • User profile
  • Data they can access
  • Purpose
  • Actions they take

Key decision

Each user builds the Analytics they need.

If each profile looks at different data, a single view cannot serve everyone. We saw a customizable experience as a major differentiator: each person arranges the dashboard around what matters most in their day-to-day work.

What we designed

We designed the solution together with the product and architecture teams.

  1. 01

    Customizable dashboards

    Users choose the KPIs they want to follow and arrange the dashboard around their routine.

  2. 02

    No-code building

    Drag and drop: the dashboard is built and rearranged by moving cards, without a single line of code.

  3. 03

    Tailored datasets

    The dataset download experience is customizable too, so data can be taken into other analyses.

Where customization stops

Users customize the dashboard, their favorites and dataset extraction. Data treatment stays with the company, and the tool suggests the right chart for each piece of data.

Learnings

Changing a paradigm takes architecture and engineering from day one. We knew it would be possible to customize both data and charts. Constant alignment with these two teams showed that hyper-customization can have impacts that are not always positive.

Customize the experience, not the data. So the dashboard, favorites and dataset extraction are customizable, but the data is treated by the company and the tool suggests the right chart. What matters is making sure users get the data and insight they need, without the risk of receiving data they cannot interpret.
